how

Three steps, once

01

Install the desktop app

On Windows, macOS or Linux. It creates a virtual camera your other apps can pick.

02

Open Webcamo on the phone

Then plug in the cable. Webcamo finds the phone and starts the stream by itself.

03

Pick it in your call

Choose “Mobile Webcam” from the camera list in Meet, Zoom or OBS. Done.

download

Open source and private. The desktop app for your computer, and the app for your phone.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)

Download installer (.exe) Portable zip (.zip)
winget install mohamedjs.webcamo

Windows SmartScreen may warn on new releases: click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS

Your computer

macOS 12 Monterey or later

Apple Silicon (M1–M4) Intel Mac
brew install mohamedjs/tap/webcamo

Works with OBS Virtual Camera.

Linux

Your computer

Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora and Arch (64-bit)

Download .deb package Universal tarball (.tar.gz)
sudo apt install ./webcamo_*_amd64.deb

Creates the virtual camera /dev/video9 (v4l2loopback) and a PipeWire microphone.

Android

Your phone

Android 8.0 or later

Camera2 with the hardware encoder, over adb (USB debugging).

Download the Android APK

Turn on USB debugging in Developer options, then plug in the cable.

iPhone

Your phone

iOS 16.4 or later

AVFoundation capture, VideoToolbox hardware encoder, over usbmuxd.

Build it from the source (Xcode)

Plug the iPhone in with a Lightning or USB-C cable and tap Trust This Computer.

why it works

Made for long calls

No flaky Wi-Fi, no account, no cloud in the middle.

A cable, not Wi-Fi

The desktop pulls the stream from the phone over USB: no dropouts from a busy network, and the phone charges during the call.

Private by design

Everything stays on 127.0.0.1 between the phone and your computer. No analytics, no account, no internet needed.

Hardware encoding

The phone encodes H.264 in hardware; the desktop writes it to a normal virtual camera every app can use.

Change it while you're live

Resolution, frame rate, lens, zoom, exposure and rotation from the desktop; the camera stays in your call.

Recovers by itself

Cable pulled or app reopened: it reconnects. If the phone gets very hot, it lowers the quality, then restores it once the phone cools.

A desktop app for every OS

A dashboard with a live preview of what your call sees, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

questions

Questions people ask

Why a USB cable instead of Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi drops packets and adds delay spikes, and it drains the phone. Over the cable the stream is steady, the phone charges, and nothing leaves your desk.

Does Webcamo send any video to the internet?

No. It runs only between your phone and your computer over 127.0.0.1 and needs no internet connection.

How much delay is there?

About 0.3 to 0.4 seconds from the camera to your call, measured with a real iPhone. 30 fps is plenty for Meet and Zoom; 60 fps is smoother for recording.

Will my phone get hot?

The phone uses its hardware encoder, so the load stays low. If it does get very hot, Webcamo lowers the quality and restores it once the phone cools down.

Can I use the phone's microphone?

On iPhone, yes: allow the microphone for Webcamo and it appears as a virtual microphone on your computer. Android sends video only for now.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything that uses a camera: Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Discord, OBS Studio and browsers.
