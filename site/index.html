Frequently Asked Questions

Why USB cable instead of Wi-Fi?
Wi-Fi connections suffer from packet loss, channel interference, and latency spikes. A USB cable guarantees continuous 50ms latency, zero dropped frames, and keeps your phone fully charged.
Does Webcamo send any video or data to the cloud?
Never. Webcamo operates strictly over local loopback (127.0.0.1) between your phone and your computer. It requires zero internet access to stream.
Will my phone overheat during long calls?
Webcamo utilizes dedicated on-chip hardware encoding (MediaCodec on Android, VideoToolbox on iOS) and automatically sleeps the phone screen while keeping the camera sensor active.
Can I use the phone's microphone for meetings?
Yes! Webcamo captures synchronized high-fidelity AAC audio from your phone and registers a virtual microphone on your operating system (PipeWire on Linux, BlackHole on macOS, VB-Cable on Windows).
Which video apps are supported?
Because Webcamo exposes standard system virtual camera and audio devices, it works automatically in Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Discord, OBS Studio, and WebRTC browsers.
